Frequently Asked Questions.

Is Count Part of the ChurchOps App?+

Count is a separate, focused app for attendance. It connects to the participating church’s staff portal attendance records. It is not currently included as a public download with a ChurchOps subscription.

Can Volunteers Help Count?+

The app supports personal invitations issued by authorized staff. An invitation is limited to a service and approved counting areas, and can expire or be revoked.

Does Count Work Offline?+

An internet connection is required to save counts. Count shows confirmed server information; there is no offline submission queue.

What Do the Totals Represent?+

Combined service totals are attendances, not unique people. Chairs and online viewers are separate from the in-person attendance total.

Where Can I Get Support?+

Contact your service coordinator for service access or an invitation. For product questions, email hello@church-ops.com. The billing and in-app account-deletion directions on the existing support page refer to the original ChurchOps app.
