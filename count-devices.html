What to Know Before You Start+

Who It’s For

For service coordinators and invited volunteers recording attendance by area and reviewing the service total.

Getting Access

The current connection is configured for Crosswalk. Staff need the appropriate church membership and attendance permissions; volunteers use a named, service-specific invitation.

Internet & Syncing

Internet is required to save counts and receive confirmed shared totals. There is no offline submission queue. Plan device and network coverage before the service.

How the Experiences Connect

Count is a separate app. The related browser attendance and reporting tools are part of Hub’s Staff Portal and use the same configured attendance records.
