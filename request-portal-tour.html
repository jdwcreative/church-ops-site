Inside ChurchOps Hub

From a Request to a Clear Next Step.

Explore the actual submission forms with fictional examples. After submitting through your church’s link, use its Request Portal to follow your own history.

01 / Invoice

Give Finance the Full Picture.

A vendor can provide contact details, invoice information, a payment preference, and supporting documentation. The church receives the context it needs to review the request.

  • Identify the vendor and the invoice being submitted.
  • Include the amount, due date, and reason for the charge.
  • Attach an invoice or supporting files when needed.

02 / Reimbursement

Put the Expense and Its Purpose Together.

A volunteer or staff member can explain an expense and provide the supporting receipt. A clear request helps the church review what was purchased and why.

  • Include the requester’s contact information.
  • Describe the ministry purpose and amount.
  • Provide receipts and the requested payment information.
Open Full-Size Screen ↗

03 / Event

Start With the Details That Matter.

A facility request gives the church a starting point for reviewing the event, expected attendance, location, and timing. Submission begins the review; it does not reserve the space.

  • Describe the event and expected attendance.
  • Share proposed dates, times, and facility needs.
  • Wait for the church’s decision before treating an event as confirmed.
