Before You Get Started.

Can We Buy Live Today?

Live is in private preview. Public signup, checkout, and App Store distribution are not yet available. The pricing page shows a proposed launch model, and our team can discuss a pilot.

Does Live Replace Our Mixer or Presentation Software?

Live brings assignments, screens, and supported status information together. It does not replace your mixing console or presentation software. ProPresenter and OBS connections are read only.

Does a Hub Subscription Include Live?

Current Hub subscriptions cover the Hub app. Live access is separate. Our Complete Suite package is a proposal for future availability, not an entitlement included with an existing purchase.
