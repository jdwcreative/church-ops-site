What to Know Before You Start+

Who It’s For

For production operators and service teams coordinating people, equipment, and screens in a room.

Getting Access

Begin with a preview conversation about your room, operator Mac, screens, and equipment. Local meter and production connections depend on compatible hardware and network access.

Internet & Syncing

Internet is required for editing and normal screen authorization, including HDMI. Local hardware connections also require the appropriate network connection.

How the Experiences Connect

A browser team view is a display for the people serving. Local production connections and desktop screen controls belong to the Mac setup. The preview can prepare a separate, read-only local team view before a service for up to six hours; it requires advance pairing and does not provide offline editing.

Questions & Answers

Can We Buy Live Today?

Live is in private preview. Public signup, checkout, and App Store distribution are not yet available. The pricing page shows a proposed launch model, and our team can discuss a pilot.

Does Live Replace Our Mixer or Presentation Software?

Live brings assignments, screens, and supported status information together. It does not replace your mixing console or presentation software. ProPresenter and OBS connections are read only.

Does a Hub Subscription Include Live?

Hub on its own does not include Live. In the proposed model, you can add Live Basic or Pro, choose Live independently, or include Live Pro through Hub’s All Products Add-on. Sound Level Monitoring is a separate optional Live add-on. Existing subscriptions and preview access do not change.

Will Live Change Our Planning Center Schedule?

Roster imports are read only. Equipment assignments stay in Live; service scheduling remains in Planning Center.

Can We Use Our Existing Screens?

Live includes browser-based team views alongside its Mac preview. The right arrangement depends on your displays, devices, network, and plan. Share your room setup so we can review compatibility.

Are the Audio Meters Real?

Supported console connections provide actual input meter readings. Sound Level Monitoring is a separate room measurement feature using a measurement microphone and calibration. The website examples use fictional readings. Console meters do not measure room decibels.
