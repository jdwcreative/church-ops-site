Requests & Follow-Up

Hub’s Request Portal gives the people working with your church a clear path from submission to follow-up. Church-specific forms and a requester portal help keep invoices, reimbursements, and event requests connected.

Give Finance the Full Picture.

A vendor can provide contact details, invoice information, a payment preference, and supporting documentation. The church receives the context it needs to review the request.

  • Identify the vendor and the invoice being submitted.
  • Include the amount, due date, and reason for the charge.
  • Attach an invoice or supporting files when needed.

Put the Expense and Its Purpose Together.

A volunteer or staff member can explain an expense and provide the supporting receipt. A clear request helps the church review what was purchased and why.

  • Include the requester’s contact information.
  • Describe the ministry purpose and amount.
  • Provide receipts and the requested payment information.

Start With the Details That Matter.

A facility request gives the church a starting point for reviewing the event, expected attendance, location, and timing. Submission begins the review; it does not reserve the space.

  • Describe the event and expected attendance.
  • Share proposed dates, times, and facility needs.
  • Wait for the church’s decision before treating an event as confirmed.

What to Know Before You Start+

Who It’s For

For vendors, volunteers, and other requesters submitting information to a church and following their own requests.

Getting Access

Start with the receiving church’s form link. For request history, use the email associated with your submission and complete the email-code sign-in.

Internet & Syncing

An internet connection is required. The current installation serves Crosswalk; other churches need their own configured Hub access.

How the Experiences Connect

The Request Portal is part of Hub. Sending a request begins the church’s review; it does not confirm approval, payment, or a facility reservation.

Questions & Answers

Where Do I Submit a New Invoice or Request?+

Use the invoice, reimbursement, or facility request link supplied by your church. The resource directory explains each form and can help you open a link you already have.

Is This the Same Login as the Staff Portal?+

No. The external Request Portal has its own email-code sign-in flow. Staff access and requester access serve different purposes.

Does Approval Mean a Payment Has Been Made?+

No. Review, approval, and payment are separate steps. Use the available request status and contact your church’s finance team when you need payment confirmation.

Who Should I Contact About My Request?+

Contact the church receiving your request for its processing, payment, or event decision. For a ChurchOps product issue, email hello@church-ops.com.

Do I Need to Install the Hub App?

External requesters use browser forms and the Request Portal’s email-code sign-in. They do not need a staff workspace to follow their own requests.

Why Do I Need a Church-Specific Form Link?

The link carries the church or team details needed to route your request. Ask the receiving church for its complete link instead of starting from an unconfigured form.

What If I Used the Wrong Email?

Contact the receiving church for help correcting or locating your request. Sign in with the email associated with the submission to see the relevant history.
