Which Devices Can We Use?

Hub has native iPhone, iPad, and Mac apps, plus its web portals. Live has a Mac preview and browser access. Music has an invited iPhone app and browser workspace. Count is in development for iPhone and iPad, with connected reporting in Hub’s Staff Portal.

See each product’s device guide: Hub, Live, Music, and Count.

Can Our Team Use Windows?

Windows users can access Hub’s configured web portals, Music’s invited browser workspace, and Live’s online workspace and shared views. Count’s connected browser reporting is in Hub’s Staff Portal. Native Windows desktop apps have not been announced; browser features and account requirements vary by product.

Is There an Apple Watch App?

A Hub companion for a paired iPhone has been developed, but public release availability is still being confirmed. Live, Music, and Count do not currently offer Watch apps. Contact us about Hub’s Watch availability before choosing a Watch-specific workflow.

Does One Login Work Everywhere?

Access varies by product. Music has its own authentication and permissions; Live access is separate; external requesters use an email-code flow. Your church’s invitation and each product’s access instructions are the right starting point.

Can Everyone See the Same Information?

No. Membership, roles, assignments, and product-specific permissions control access. Sensitive staff tools require additional authorization. A songwriting credit also does not automatically grant song access.

Where Do Staff and Vendors Sign In?

The Resources directory has the current app, staff, and requester destinations. External requesters should use the receiving church’s form link and the email associated with their submission.

What If an Invitation or Form Link Does Not Work?

Use the complete link you received, including its church or service details. Ask the church administrator or service coordinator for a replacement if it has expired or been revoked. The form link helper can open an existing ChurchOps form link.
