What to Know Before You Start+

Who It’s For

For production operators and service teams coordinating people, equipment, and screens in a room.

Getting Access

Begin with a preview conversation about your room, operator Mac, screens, and equipment. Local meter and production connections depend on compatible hardware and network access.

Internet & Syncing

Internet is required for editing and normal screen authorization, including HDMI. Local hardware connections also require the appropriate network connection.

How the Experiences Connect

A browser team view is a display for the people serving. Local production connections and desktop screen controls belong to the Mac setup. The preview can prepare a separate, read-only local team view before a service for up to six hours; it requires advance pairing and does not provide offline editing.
