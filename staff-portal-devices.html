What to Know Before You Start+

Who It’s For

For church staff handling everyday work and leaders using the administration tools their role permits.

Getting Access

Use the staff account and portal link provided by your church. Sensitive employment, pay, and administration tools require additional permissions.

Internet & Syncing

An internet connection is required. The current installation serves Crosswalk; other churches need their own configured Hub access.

How the Experiences Connect

The Staff Portal is part of Hub, with browser workflows shaped around staff roles. Hub’s separate native apps have their own device requirements.
