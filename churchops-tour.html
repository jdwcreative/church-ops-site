Inside the Product

Your Day, in View.

A closer look at the native workspace your team can carry with them.

01 / Work

Start With What Needs You.

Open Work for a clear route into approvals, requests, budgets, and your schedule. The workspace brings recurring responsibilities into one familiar place.

  • Find assigned approvals and incoming requests.
  • Move from a task to its supporting details.
  • See the tools available to your team and role.

02 / Time

Keep the Day on Record.

Clock in, review logs, and prepare a timesheet without moving to a separate timekeeping tool. Manual entries and time-off tools keep exceptions close to the everyday flow.

  • Start and end a shift from the Time screen.
  • Review logs before submitting a timesheet.
  • Use manual entries or time-off requests when needed.
