    The Same Products. Your Choice of Package.

    Individual Live, Music, and Count prices are the same whether you add them to Hub or choose them separately. The All Products Add-on discounts all three together when paired with any Hub tier. A complete suite is your selected Hub tier plus that package.

    Examples: Hub Team + All Products is $148.99/month or $1,489.99/year. Hub Growth + All Products is $218.99/month or $1,689.99/year. Sound Level Monitoring is extra in both cases.

    Before You Choose

    What Can We Purchase Today?

    Only Hub subscriptions are available for purchase. Live is in private preview, Music serves invited teams, and Count is in development. The calculator previews a future commercial model; it does not activate access or collect payments. Existing invited-team Music access remains free.

    Does the Suite Include Every Add-on?

    The All Products Add-on includes Live Pro (3 rooms, 3 operators), Music (1 workspace, 10 collaborators), and Count (1 campus). Staff and Request Portals are included through Hub. Sound Level Monitoring and additional capacity are priced separately; future specialized add-ons are not automatically included.

    How Are Larger Setups Priced?

    Proposed Live Pro capacity additions are $12/operator/month or $120/year and $15/room/month or $150/year. Sound Level Monitoring adds $9/month or $90/year for each monitored room, including an additional Live room. More Music collaborators, Count campuses, or Hub seats need a scoped proposal. The calculator covers standard plan capacities.

    Will Our Existing Subscriptions Change?

    No. Any move from an App Store subscription to a future bundle needs a confirmed transition to avoid duplicate billing. Products currently have separate accounts and permissions; a package does not promise one login or automatic data sharing.

    Can Count Be Purchased Without Hub?

    That is part of the proposed commercial model. Count is still in development, and its current connected reporting uses Hub’s Staff Portal. Standalone onboarding and reporting must be confirmed before any separate Count subscription is offered.
