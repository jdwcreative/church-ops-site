How the Suite Would Work

The proposed monthly bundle is $37.99 less than Hub Team plus Live Pro, Music, and Count ($186.99). One church would choose the complete package or a smaller selection of products. Existing App Store subscriptions would need a confirmed transition before any bundle begins; there is no automatic conversion or second charge here.

Products currently have separate accounts and permissions. A suite package would simplify purchasing; it does not imply a shared login or automatic data exchange today.
