A Closer Look at the Workspace.

A browser workspace shaped around the work your staff is authorized to do.

01 / My Workspace

A Familiar Place to Begin.

The staff workspace brings personal tools and church resources into a browser. Staff can start with their own responsibilities, then move to the areas their role permits.

  • Find personal resources and everyday staff tools.
  • Move into timekeeping, finance, or attendance when authorized.
  • Use the church’s established staff account and access.

02 / People

Keep Staff Administration Organized.

Authorized leaders can review employment records and the history behind changes. The example shows a fictional staff roster in the Executive workspace.

  • Review staff employment information in context.
  • Keep history and draft changes visible to authorized users.
  • Sensitive employment and pay tools require additional access.
