Inside the Product

A Count You Can Follow.

See how a service moves from a volunteer’s count to a reviewed attendance picture.

01 / Overview

Know Which Service You’re In.

The overview gives the coordinator a service-specific picture of the count. Area progress helps show what has been covered before the total is reviewed.

  • Keep counts tied to the selected service.
  • See area progress in one place.
  • Use named, service-scoped participation.
Open Full-Size Screen ↗

02 / Count

One Space. One Clear Task.

A focused counter gives a volunteer a large tap target and simple corrections. The area name and service stay visible while the count is being recorded.

  • Tap to add a person or enter a total.
  • Correct a count with Remove One.
  • Follow the church’s rules for who belongs in each area.
Open Full-Size Screen ↗

03 / Review

Check the Parts Behind the Total.

Review the attendance breakdown before confirming it. Each area stays visible so a coordinator can spot a missing count or an unexpected number.

  • Review rooms, teams, and children’s areas together.
  • Check that each person is counted once.
  • Keep chairs and online viewers separate from in-person totals.
Open Full-Size Screen ↗

04 / Snapshot

Keep the Service in Context.

A service snapshot brings the result back into a concise overview. It helps the team read the count alongside the service it belongs to.

  • Return to the selected service’s recorded picture.
  • Use the breakdown to support follow-up.
  • Treat attendance totals as visits, not unique people across services.
