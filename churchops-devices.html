What to Know Before You Start+

Who It’s For

For staff, administrators, and board members who need finance, people, time, and planning in one workspace.

Getting Access

Start with your church’s team invitation and an active Hub subscription. Portal access is configured for each church; the current installations serve Crosswalk.

Internet & Syncing

Use an internet connection for shared updates, requests, and team access. Available tools depend on your role, app version, and church setup.

How the Experiences Connect

The Staff and Request Portals are features of Hub. Browser access provides the relevant portal workflows; it does not imply every native app feature is available on the web.
