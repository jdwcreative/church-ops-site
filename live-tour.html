Inside the Product

Walk Into a Ready Room.

Follow a service from the team roster to the checks before the gathering.

01 / Services

People and Gear, Together.

The service workspace puts each person beside their microphone, in-ear monitor, and input. Your operator can follow assignments without piecing together separate lists.

  • Bring in the selected Planning Center service roster.
  • Spot required in-ear assignments and equipment conflicts.
  • Read supported console levels beside the relevant source.
Open Full-Size Screen ↗

02 / Readiness

Check the Room Before It Fills.

A room check brings the selected service, saved team, console connection, equipment mapping, and screens into one view. It helps the operator see which details need a second look.

  • Confirm the service and roster you intend to use.
  • Distinguish fresh readings from a connection that needs attention.
  • Review saved devices and displays before sending a view.
Open Full-Size Screen ↗

03 / Screens

Give Each Screen a Purpose.

Prepare a focused team view for the people who need it. Choose which people and information appear on a backstage display, an operator screen, or a team member’s device.

  • Save a view for a specific team or position.
  • Keep the screen focused on useful assignments.
  • Check the room, display, and plan before rollout.
