Frequently Asked Questions.

Where Do I Submit a New Invoice or Request?+

Use the invoice, reimbursement, or facility request link supplied by your church. The resource directory explains each form and can help you open a link you already have.

Is This the Same Login as the Staff Portal?+

No. The external Request Portal has its own email-code sign-in flow. Staff access and requester access serve different purposes.

Does Approval Mean a Payment Has Been Made?+

No. Review, approval, and payment are separate steps. Use the available request status and contact your church’s finance team when you need payment confirmation.

Who Should I Contact About My Request?+

Contact the church receiving your request for its processing, payment, or event decision. For a ChurchOps product issue, email hello@church-ops.com.

Do I Need to Install the Hub App?

External requesters use browser forms and the Request Portal’s email-code sign-in. They do not need a staff workspace to follow their own requests.

Why Do I Need a Church-Specific Form Link?

The link carries the church or team details needed to route your request. Ask the receiving church for its complete link instead of starting from an unconfigured form.

What If I Used the Wrong Email?

Contact the receiving church for help correcting or locating your request. Sign in with the email associated with the submission to see the relevant history.
