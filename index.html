Before You Get Started

All Questions ↗
Can We Start with One Product?

Yes. Hub is available on its own today. Live, Music, and Count have separate release and access arrangements. You do not need to commit to the whole suite.

Are the Staff and Request Portals Separate Products?

No. Both are part of ChurchOps Hub. Staff use the workspace for their authorized work; requesters use the Request Portal for their own submissions and history.

Will Everything Use One Login?

Products currently have their own accounts and permissions. Shared branding and proposed suite pricing do not mean one login or automatic sharing across every app.
