A few things to know

Clarity starts here.

Finding the right fit should feel simple.

Where should our church start?+

Start with ChurchOps for team operations. If you’re interested in the Staff Portal or attendance tools, talk to us about your church’s needs and current availability.

Are all the products available today?+

ChurchOps is listed on the App Store. The Staff Portal is currently configured for participating church staff, with the existing Crosswalk portal in use. ChurchOps Count is in development and is not yet available for public download.

How do subscriptions work?+

ChurchOps operational workflows require an active paid team subscription. See current plans in the app’s Billing settings or on the App Store listing. Portal and Count access should be discussed separately; a ChurchOps subscription does not promise access to every product.

Already using a ChurchOps tool?+

Visit Resources for app access, existing portal links, help with church-specific forms, and support. Your existing invitation and form links still lead to the same workflows.
