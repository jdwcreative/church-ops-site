Present for the people

A Simple Rhythm
for Service Day.

01

Open the Right Service.

Use the service schedule connected by your church. Staff sign in, and invited volunteers use a personal service invitation.

02

Count Your Space.

Tap to count, enter a total, or adjust with plus and minus. Review empty spaces, track chairs, and add notes where needed.

03

Confirm the Picture.

Review the areas, confirm your name, and finalize the service. Shared snapshots help the team see the day together.

Many Counters.

Count & Snapshot

Two focused views: record the attendance in front of you, then see the shared picture across the day’s services.

Named Participation

Staff and invited volunteers work within their authorized service and areas, with each change attributed to a counter.

Review Before Finalizing

Every area needs review. Finalization includes a named confirmation and a retained submission receipt.

Connected Reporting

Uses the same attendance records as the participating church portal, with Planning Center service occurrences and a configured Google Drive export.

Preparing for What’s Next.

ChurchOps Count is a separate app in development. Public App Store and TestFlight distribution have not yet begun.

Its current connection is configured for Crosswalk. A participating church account or a staff-issued service invitation will be required. An internet connection is required to save attendance.
