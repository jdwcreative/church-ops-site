Inside the Product

Follow the Life of a Song.

One place for the work around the song—from a rough idea to a shared listen.

01 / Song Board

See What’s Moving.

Keep songs organized through Ideas, Writing, Recording, Mixing, and Finished. The board gives collaborators a shared starting point for the work they can access.

  • Find songs by their current stage.
  • Keep original ideas and work in progress together.
  • Open a song for its audio, lyrics, notes, and plan.

02 / Listen

Leave a Note at the Moment.

Play a version, mark a moment, and put feedback where a collaborator can understand it. Keep discussion connected to the recording instead of a separate message thread.

  • Compare the recordings attached to a song.
  • Add timestamped feedback with Mark Moment.
  • Return to the musical moment behind a note.
Open Full-Size Screen ↗

03 / Lyrics

Give the Song Its Shape.

Write and arrange lyrics in sections. Verse, chorus, bridge, and tag labels make the structure easier to follow as the team works through the next version.

  • Organize lyrics into meaningful sections.
  • Arrange the structure as the song develops.
  • Request review from the teammates you choose.
Open Full-Size Screen ↗

04 / Plans

Make Room for the Next Step.

Keep song-related plans visible in a calendar or an upcoming list. A rehearsal or writing session stays connected to the song it is moving forward.

  • See dated, undated, and completed plans.
  • Filter for the plans that involve you.
  • Add a copy of a plan to Apple Calendar.

05 / Player

Keep Listening Beyond the Desk.

The Music player keeps the track and lyrics together, with playback controls and a listening queue. Download available audio ahead of time when you need to listen offline.

  • Listen with lyrics close at hand.
  • Favorite a track and move through the queue.
  • Use downloaded audio for offline listening.

A Workflow Built Around Songs

Keep the Song Moving.
Keep the Team Together.

01

Catch the Idea.

Give each song a home. Track its stage from idea through writing, recording, mixing, and finished, with tags and a clear view of what’s next.

02

Write and Arrange.

Build lyrics in sections, keep notes with the song, and bring collaborators into the same workspace. Share with selected teammates when you’re ready.

03

Listen and Respond.

Keep multiple recordings together. Mark a moment with timestamped feedback, then request a review of the version you want your team to hear.

04

Plan the Next Step.

Organize rehearsals, recordings, videos, and release plans. Keep writing and recording credits alongside the work without confusing credits with access.

Frequently Asked Questions.

How Does Our Team Get Access?

Music currently serves the existing invited team. A team administrator provides accounts; downloading an app does not create a workspace or grant access to songs. Contact us to discuss future team availability.

Do We Need a ChurchOps Hub Subscription?

No. Music is currently free for the invited team and does not require Hub. The future paid team plan on our pricing page is a proposal; it does not change existing access or create a charge.

Where Can Existing Music Users Get Help?

Use Music Support or email justindwampler@gmail.com. Read the separate Music Privacy Policy for Music’s storage and account practices.

Can Everyone on the Team Open Every Song?

Song access is controlled separately from a writing credit. Listing someone as a writer does not itself grant access to the song; use the app’s sharing and team access tools.

Can We Keep Several Recordings of a Song?

Yes. A song can hold multiple audio versions so the team can listen and discuss the work in context.

Can We Listen Offline?

Available audio can be downloaded in advance for offline listening. Download it while connected; other collaboration and syncing actions still need a connection.
