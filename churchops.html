Frequently Asked Questions.

How Do I Join My Church’s Team?+

Use the invitation your church administrator sends you. The existing ChurchOps invitation page guides you through installing the app and opening your invitation. If you need a fresh invitation, contact your administrator.

Where Do I Find Pricing and Billing?+

Current subscription options are shown in the app and on the App Store. Operational access requires an active paid team subscription, and seat limits depend on the plan. For purchase restoration or billing help, visit Support.

How Do Hub’s Apps and Portals Work Together?+

The native apps, Staff Portal, and Request Portal are all parts of Hub. Staff use ChurchOps account credentials, with church staff access verified separately; external requesters use the requester email-code flow. Your church must provision portal access; installing the app alone does not grant access.

How Do Account Deletion and Privacy Work?+

The original ChurchOps app provides Settings → Delete Account. Deletion does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Read the existing Privacy Policy, Terms, and Support pages for the full details.

Does Hub Run Payroll?

Hub supports time records, timesheets, and related staff workflows. Pay estimates and records are not a payroll payment service. Confirm your church’s payroll process with its administrator.

Who Can See Finance and Staff Information?

Team membership and assigned roles determine available tools. Your church should set up its access and approval responsibilities before inviting the wider team.

Where Should a New Church Start?

Start with the people and one recurring workflow you want to organize, such as expenses or timekeeping. We can discuss team setup, the right seat count, and the parts of the suite that fit your church.
