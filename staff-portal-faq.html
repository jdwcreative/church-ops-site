Before you sign in

Frequently Asked Questions.

Can Anyone Create a Staff Portal Account?+

No. Staff access must be provided by a participating church. The existing portal verifies church staff membership in addition to account sign-in.

I Already Work at Crosswalk. Where Do I Go?+

Use the existing Crosswalk staff sign-in with your ChurchOps email and password. For church information and public resources, visit Crosswalk Staff.

Does a ChurchOps Subscription Include a Portal?+

Yes. The Staff Portal is a feature of ChurchOps Hub. Your church still needs its portal configured and your staff access provisioned; downloading the app alone does not complete that setup. Contact us to discuss your church’s needs and current availability.

How Do I Get Help with a Form or Staff Access?+

For an invitation, role, or church-specific link, start with your church administrator. For a product issue, email hello@church-ops.com with your account email, church name, and a short description.

Can Every Manager See Employment and Pay Records?

No. Sensitive employment tools are restricted. The Executive workspace uses additional authorization and a PIN; ordinary staff access does not grant access to every employee record.

Is This Ready for Any Church to Sign Up?

The current portal is configured for Crosswalk. Another church’s setup, access rules, and rollout need to be reviewed with us before its staff begins using a portal.

How Does the Staff Portal Fit Within Hub?

The Staff Portal is part of ChurchOps Hub and supports its staff and operational workflows. Music and Live have their own product access. A shared brand does not mean every app uses the same account or permissions.
