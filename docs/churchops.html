Get Started With HubApps & Downloads
Which Devices Can We Use?

Hub has native iPhone, iPad, and Mac apps. iPhone and iPad require iOS/iPadOS 18.2 or later. Staff and Request Portals work in a browser, including on Windows. Browser workflows vary from the native apps.

A paired-iPhone Watch companion has been developed; confirm public availability before planning a Watch-specific workflow.

Find Apps and Device Details
Does Hub Run Payroll?

Hub supports time records, timesheets, and related staff workflows. Pay estimates and records are not a payroll payment service. Confirm the payroll process with your church administrator.

How Do Billing and Access Work?

Create your account and church on the website, then get Hub and sign in with that same account. Select your church and open Settings → Billing to choose monthly or annual billing and confirm with Apple. One Hub plan covers the church’s Hub access within its member limit, including the Staff and Request Portals. Live, Music, and Count are separate products.
