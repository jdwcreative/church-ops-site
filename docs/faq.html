Common Questions

Choose With Clarity.

Practical answers about the products, access, and getting your church started.

Which Product Should We Start With?

Hub covers daily church operations. Live supports team preparation, service sets, lighting, and room displays. Music is for original songwriting and collaboration. Count focuses on service attendance.

Compare the Products
What Can We Use Today?

Hub is available for iPhone, iPad, and Mac with an active paid team subscription, plus configured Staff and Request Portals. Live is in private preview, Music serves invited teams, and Count is coming soon. Each product page explains its access arrangements.

Are the Portals Separate Products?

No. Staff Portal and Request Portal are features of Hub. Staff use their authorized browser workspace. External requesters use church-specific forms and email-code access to their own submissions.

Does One Login Work Everywhere?

Your Hub account connects its native app and Staff Portal. Select the right church before opening its workspace. Live and Music have separate access arrangements; external requesters use an email-code flow. Branding or a proposed package does not automatically grant product or church access.

Can We See How a Product Works?

Each product page contains actual interface examples with labeled sample data and a practical workflow. The illustrative Live display uses fictional people and simulated readings.

Explore Hub’s Example
Where Do We Buy Hub?

Hub subscriptions are purchased inside the app through Apple. Creating a website account does not purchase a subscription. Monthly and annual totals are shown on the pricing page; Apple confirms the final local price and applicable taxes.

Are Setup and Migration Included?

Setup, migration, optional connections, and custom work are scoped separately. Tell us about your existing tools and intended rollout before agreeing on that work.

Do We Need the Complete Suite?

No. Hub can be used on its own. Proposed product and bundle pricing is a future commercial model; it does not open checkout or activate access. Existing invited-team Music access remains free.

Which Devices Can We Use?

Device and browser support differ by product. The Downloads page brings current app links, access instructions, and compatibility details together.

Open Apps & Downloads
Who Can See Church Information?

Membership, assigned roles, and product-specific permissions determine access. A Hub account does not grant membership in another church. Sensitive staff tools require additional authorization.

How Do Staff and Requesters Get Access?

Staff use their invitation and Hub account. Vendors and other requesters use the receiving church’s link and the email associated with their request.

Where Can We Get Help?

Use product support for an existing product issue. Your church handles invitations, permissions, payment decisions, and event decisions. For product selection or setup, contact ChurchOps.
