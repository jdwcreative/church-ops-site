Devices & Connection Requirements

The current iPhone and iPad builds require iOS/iPadOS 18.2 or later. Internet is required to save attendance. Authorized staff can review connected reports in Hub’s Staff Portal; there is no separate Count web or desktop app.

Proposed Pricing

Proposed Count pricing is $19/month or $190/year per campus. Standalone onboarding and reporting remain to be confirmed before a separate Count subscription is offered.

Preview Count Pricing
Can Volunteers Help Count?

Invited volunteers can participate through personal service invitations. Their access is limited to the appropriate service and areas.

Can Count Work Offline?

An internet connection is required to save attendance in the current shared workflow. Plan connectivity with your attendance coordinator.
