Ask About Your Team
Who Counts as a Team Member?

A team member is a place within your Hub plan’s capacity. Choose a plan for 3, 10, or 25 members. Ask us about your staff, administrators, and requester roles if you are unsure which people need seats.

What If We Need More Than 25 Members?

Tell us your team size and the workflows you need. We will discuss a scoped arrangement rather than assuming one setup fits every church.
