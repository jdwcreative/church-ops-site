Choosing a Plan

Who Counts as a Team Member?

A seat is a team-member place within your Hub plan limit. If you are unsure how your staff roles fit, tell us about your team before choosing.

What If We Need More Than 25 Members?

Ask about your church’s needs, including staff size and campuses. Larger setups need a scoped proposal.

Do We Need the Other Products?

No. You can use Hub on its own. Live, Music and Count are optional; their product pages explain current availability.
