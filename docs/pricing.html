Before You Choose.

Every Hub plan has the same core features. Choose your team size and billing period.

Does Annual Billing Include More Products?

No. Monthly and annual billing are two ways to pay for the same Hub plan. Neither includes Live, Music, or Count. Staff and Request Portals are included with Hub.

Who Needs to Buy a Plan?

An authorized church owner or administrator activates the church’s Hub plan. Invited staff join their church and do not buy separate Hub subscriptions.

Who Counts as a Team Member?

Choose capacity for 3, 10, or 25 team members. Ask us about your staff, administrators, and requester roles if you are unsure which people need seats.

Already Paid for Hub?

Use the ChurchOps account, church, and Apple purchase account associated with your existing subscription. Open Billing in Hub and use Restore Purchases if needed. Check the existing subscription before starting another.
