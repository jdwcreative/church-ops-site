Start With the Receiving Church.

Church owners enable Request Portal access and sender settings before sharing links. Requesters use email-code access to their own submissions.

Which Devices Can Open the Portal?

The portal runs in your browser on a phone, tablet, Mac, or Windows computer. No separate portal app is required. Browser tools vary from the native Hub apps.

How Do I Get Access?

Use the link supplied by the receiving church and the email associated with your submission. Ask the church for the right link if you do not have one.
