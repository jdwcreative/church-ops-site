Before You Install.

Hub Device Requirements

iPhone and iPad require iOS/iPadOS 18.2 or later. Check the Mac App Store listing for current Mac compatibility. Windows users can access configured browser portals. A Hub Watch companion has been developed; confirm public release availability before relying on it.

Live Device Requirements

The current Mac preview requires macOS 13 or later. Browser access and team views depend on your Live setup. Confirm mixer, equipment, and local-network compatibility before rollout. Native Windows and mobile apps have not been announced.

Music Device Requirements

The invited iPhone build requires iOS 17 or later. Use the browser workspace on Mac, Windows, and iPad. Other native apps have not been announced.

Count Device Requirements

The current prerelease iPhone and iPad builds require iOS/iPadOS 18.2 or later. An internet connection is required to save attendance. Reporting currently connects through Hub’s Staff Portal.
