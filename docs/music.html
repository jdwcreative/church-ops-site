Ask About Music Access
Already Using Music?

Use your administrator-provided app or workspace. For a product issue, open Music Support or email justindwampler@gmail.com.

Music Privacy Policy
Devices & Connection Requirements

The current iPhone build requires iOS 17 or later. The invited browser workspace is available on Mac, Windows, and iPad. Native iPad, Mac, Windows, and Watch apps have not been announced.

Pricing & Availability

Current invited-team Music access is free. A Hub subscription does not create a Music account. A public paid Music plan is not available to purchase.

See Product Costs & Access
Do We Need Hub to Use Music?

No. Music is a distinct product with its own invited-team access arrangements. A Hub subscription does not automatically create a Music account or grant song access.

Can We Listen Offline?

Download recordings before going offline. Keep the app open after reconnecting so pending changes can sync. Offline listening does not imply every team workflow works without a connection.
