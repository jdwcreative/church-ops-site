01

For Invited Staff

Join Your Team.

Open the invitation your church administrator sent you. Sign in or create your account, complete your profile and accept the invitation. If you already have an account, use it rather than creating a second one.

Choose the church you want to work in before opening its portal. If you belong to more than one church, check the church name when switching workspaces. Your role determines which tools and records you can use.

02

For Church Owners

Make It Your Church.

  1. Create Your Church. Sign in, choose Create Church and enter your church’s name and timezone. If your church already uses ChurchOps, ask its owner for an invitation instead of creating another workspace.
  2. Complete Church Setup. Review Church Profile, choose its church address and portal display name, then enable the Staff Portal under Portal Access.
  3. Prepare the Workflows You Use. Review your Hub subscription and staff seats. Set payroll dates, timezone and policy before using time and payroll tools. Connect optional services using your church’s own authorized accounts.
  4. Invite Your Team. Invite staff and assign the appropriate roles. Review the setup status for each tool before directing your team to use it.

Each church has its own setup and subscription status. Creating an account does not purchase a plan, connect a provider or grant access to another church.

03

The App and the Browser

Open Your Church Portal.

Staff Portal

Sign in on app.church-ops.com, select your church and choose Open Web Portal. You can also use the app’s visible portal shortcut. Your church and account stay connected; protected areas may still require your personal PIN.

Request Portal

Church owners enable Request Portal access and complete its sender settings before sharing church-specific form links. Vendors and other requesters use the receiving church’s link and email-code sign-in to follow their own requests. A staff login is not required for that requester flow.

Crosswalk’s existing addresses continue to serve Crosswalk. Other churches use their own configured workspace and links.

04

Optional Custom Address

Your Address. Connected to ChurchOps.

A church-owned address, such as staff.yourchurch.org, can open your church’s workspace. Sign-in and portal pages continue on app.church-ops.com. Your church keeps its own name, branding and data.

  1. In Church Setup, open Custom Church Address and choose Manage Custom Addresses.
  2. Add a hostname you control. Copy the exact ownership and routing records shown into your domain provider’s settings. Keep unrelated website and email records unchanged.
  3. Check the address status. Ownership, connection and the secure certificate must be ready before sharing it with staff.
  4. Keep your central ChurchOps address available. It remains the fallback while a custom address is pending or needs attention.

This is an entrance to your ChurchOps workspace; it does not move your public church website. Removing a custom address stops that entrance and keeps the central workspace available.

05

When You Need a Hand

Keep Your Work Safe.

My Church Is Missing

Check the account you signed in with and open the invitation for that account. Ask your church administrator to confirm your membership. Do not create a replacement church to recover access.

My Portal or Custom Address Is Unavailable

Try your church’s central ChurchOps address. Its owner can review Portal Access and custom-address status in Church Setup. Save unfinished browser work before refreshing or signing out.

How Do I Leave or Delete My Account?

Leaving a church changes your access to that church. To delete your ChurchOps account, open Settings in the Hub app and choose Delete Account. Church-owned staff records and operational history may remain. Owners may need to transfer responsibility first; read the confirmation before proceeding. Apple subscriptions are managed separately.
