I Don’t Have a Link

Ask the receiving church for the appropriate link. ChurchOps does not select a church or create an invitation on your behalf.

My Link Has Expired or Access Is Denied

Ask the church administrator or service coordinator for a replacement. Keep the full original URL rather than removing its query details.

Does Submission Mean Approval or Payment?

No. Submission, review, approval, and payment are separate steps. A facility request is not a confirmed reservation. Contact the receiving church about its decisions or processing times.
