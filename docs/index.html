More Questions & Answers
Do We Need All Four Products?

No. Hub handles church operations on its own. Explore Live, Music, or Count when your team needs them; each has its own access stage.

What’s Included With Hub?

Every Hub plan includes core finance, staff, and planning tools, native Hub apps, and Staff and Request Portals. Live, Music, and Count are not included. Monthly and annual billing cover the same Hub features.

How Does a New Church Get Started?

Create your account and church, then open Hub with the same account and church selected. Choose and purchase your plan in Settings → Billing. Invite your team after activation. Invited staff do not buy their own Hub subscription.
