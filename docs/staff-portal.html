Your Church Workspace.

Use ChurchOps account credentials, then select your church. The owner enables portal access and configures roles before staff use the workspace.

Which Devices Can Open the Portal?

The portal runs in your browser on a phone, tablet, Mac, or Windows computer. No separate portal app is required. Browser tools vary from the native Hub apps.

How Do I Get Access?

Open your administrator’s invitation and use the invited account. If access is missing, ask the church administrator to confirm membership and permissions.
