Devices & Connection Requirements

The current Mac preview requires macOS 13 or later. Browser workspaces and shared team views work on supported computers, phones, and tablets. Song and lighting workflows need compatible local software and room setup. We’ll review equipment and fixture compatibility before live use. Native Windows and mobile apps have not been announced.

Proposed Pricing

Proposed Live Basic: $19/month for 1 room and 1 operator. Proposed Live Pro: $59/month for 3 rooms and 3 operators, with Smart Assign, independent screens, and production integrations. Public checkout is not open.

What Can Lighting Studio Help Us Do?

Develop a full-set lighting direction, choose palettes, shape song moments, and preview the room. Supported Lightkey workflows can follow prepared arrangements. Actual fixture mappings, software, connections, and physical commissioning must be reviewed for your room; this is not a promise of universal fixture support or autonomous live setup.

Does Live Replace Our Existing Tools?

Live works alongside Planning Center, your mixer, and presentation software. Planning Center roster imports and the named ProPresenter/OBS status connections are read only. Song and lighting workflows have their own compatible local setup.

Are the Meter Readings Real?

Supported mixer connections can supply actual readings in the product. This website’s illustrative team display uses fictional people and simulated meters.
