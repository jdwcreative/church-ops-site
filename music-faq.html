Frequently Asked Questions.

How Does Our Team Get Access?

Music currently serves the existing invited team. A team administrator provides accounts; downloading an app does not create a workspace or grant access to songs. Contact us to discuss future team availability.

Do We Need a ChurchOps Hub Subscription?

No. Music is currently free for the invited team and does not require Hub. The future paid team plan on our pricing page is a proposal; it does not change existing access or create a charge.

Where Can Existing Music Users Get Help?

Use Music Support or email justindwampler@gmail.com. Read the separate Music Privacy Policy for Music’s storage and account practices.

Can Everyone on the Team Open Every Song?

Song access is controlled separately from a writing credit. Listing someone as a writer does not itself grant access to the song; use the app’s sharing and team access tools.

Can We Keep Several Recordings of a Song?

Yes. A song can hold multiple audio versions so the team can listen and discuss the work in context.

Can We Listen Offline?

Available audio can be downloaded in advance for offline listening. Download it while connected; other collaboration and syncing actions still need a connection.
