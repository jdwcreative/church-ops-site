Does Live Replace Planning Center, Our Mixer, or Presentation Software?

Live complements those tools. Planning Center roster imports are read only; assignments stay in Live. Supported console connections provide status and meters. ProPresenter and OBS integrations are read only. Review your specific equipment with us before a rollout.

Can the Apps Work Offline?

It depends on the workflow. Music supports offline listening to audio downloaded in advance. Count currently requires internet for its shared workflow. Do not assume offline support for every product or action; discuss your service environment with us.

Does Submitting a Request Mean It Is Approved?

No. Submission, review, approval, and payment are distinct steps. A facility request is not a confirmed reservation. Contact the receiving church for its decision, processing time, or payment confirmation.

Who Can Help Us?

For product and setup questions, contact ChurchOps or email hello@church-ops.com. Existing Music users can find product-specific support on the Music page. Your church handles its own invitations, permissions, payments, and event decisions.
